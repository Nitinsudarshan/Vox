Regarding customer support tickets, we received seven reports regarding unexpected application crashes during sleep and wake cycles. When a laptop enters connected standby mode while a meeting recording is in progress, the Windows operating system suspends the audio capture driver without dispatching an explicit device removal event. The audio team has refactored the capture loop to wrap device polling in a robust health check that catches invalid handle errors, marks the active segment as closed, flushes pending speech buffers to disk, and automatically attempts endpoint re-initialization upon system resume. This graceful recovery pattern prevents data loss and ensures that no meeting audio is corrupted.
