Whisper transcription pipeline ke baare mein baat karein, toh segmentation logic mein jo adaptive silence threshold humne introduce kiya tha, uska impact kafi positive raha hai. Pehle kya ho raha tha ki agar speaker beech mein do second sochne ke liye rukta tha, segmenter turant hard split create kar deta tha, aur sentence aadha adhura decode hota tha. Now, the segmenter checks speech energy levels and waits for at least seven hundred milliseconds of confirmed silence before closing an utterance. Aur agar duration twenty-five seconds exceed kar jati hai, tabhi forced split flag set hota hai. Downstream decoder ko hum previous segment ka clean text as an initial prompt pass kar rahe hain, which maintains syntactic continuity across splits.
